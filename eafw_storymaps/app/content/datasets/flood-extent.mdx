    ## Flood Extent — Return Periods

    This dataset provides flood extent maps for the Greater Horn of Africa,
    derived from JRC Global Surface Water observations. The maps show areas
    likely to be inundated during flood events with return periods of 20, 50,
    100, and 200 years.

    The data is used by FloodWatch to assess flood hazard levels and identify
    populations at risk during extreme rainfall events.
