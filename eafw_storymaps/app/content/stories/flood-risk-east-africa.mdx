    ## The Greater Horn of Africa: A Region at Risk

    The Greater Horn of Africa — spanning Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, Uganda,
    South Sudan, Sudan, Rwanda, Burundi, Djibouti, Eritrea, and Tanzania —
    faces recurring flood events that threaten lives, livelihoods, and
    infrastructure.

    FloodWatch monitors these risks using satellite observations, hydrological
    models, and weather forecasts to provide early warnings.
  </Chapter>

    ## Flood Extent Mapping

    Using JRC Global Surface Water data, we map areas likely to flood during
    events of different magnitudes. The blue shading shows flood extent for
    return periods ranging from 20 years (light blue) to 200 years (dark blue).

    These maps are essential for identifying communities living in flood-prone
    areas and planning evacuation routes.
  </Chapter>

    ## Rainfall Forecasting with WRF

    The Weather Research and Forecasting (WRF) model provides daily rainfall
    predictions across the region. Heavy rainfall events often trigger flash
    floods, especially in areas with steep terrain or poor drainage.

    FloodWatch ingests WRF forecasts twice weekly, converting them to
    Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs (COGs) for rapid visualization.
  </Chapter>

    ## Population Exposure

    Combining flood extent with population density data from LandScan reveals
    where the most people are at risk. Urban areas along rivers — such as
    Nairobi, Kampala, and Addis Ababa — show high concentrations of exposed
    populations.

    This intersection of hazard and exposure is the foundation of
    FloodWatch's impact-based forecasting approach.
  </Chapter>

    ## About FloodWatch

    FloodWatch is operated by ICPAC (IGAD Climate Prediction and Applications
    Centre) to provide operational flood early warnings for the Greater Horn
    of Africa. The system integrates multiple data sources including:

    - **Hydrological forecasts** from HYPE and GloFAS models
    - **Weather forecasts** from WRF numerical weather prediction
    - **Satellite observations** from JRC Global Surface Water
    - **Population data** from LandScan and WorldPop

    All data is served through a STAC-compliant catalog and visualized using
    Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs for fast, interactive map rendering.
  </Prose>
